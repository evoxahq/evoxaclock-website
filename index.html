Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how EvoxaClock handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. About EvoxaClock

EvoxaClock is a clock utility app by Evoxa. It may include clock displays, alarms, timers, a stopwatch, world clocks, widgets, and sleep or bedtime tools. In this policy, “the app” means EvoxaClock.

2. Information we collect

EvoxaClock is designed to provide its core clock features on your device. We do not ask you to create an account to use the core features. The app does not sell your personal information.

Information you enter or choose inside the app—such as alarm settings, timer settings, selected cities, preferences, or sounds you import—may be stored locally on your device so the relevant features can work.

The app’s actual data handling depends on the features and software components included in the version you install. If a version includes optional online services, analytics, crash reporting, cloud backup, or other third-party SDKs, those components may process information as described in their own notices. Do not publish this policy until it has been checked against the final app build.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Notifications: to notify you about alarms, timers, or other reminders where supported.
  • Alarms and exact scheduling: to schedule alarms or timers at the times you set, where Android requires the relevant capability.
  • Audio or media access: if you choose a custom alarm sound, to let you select and play the sound you chose. The app should only access items permitted by Android and your selection.
  • Vibration: to provide vibration alerts when enabled.
  • Device boot or background scheduling: where supported, to restore scheduled alarms after the device restarts.

Permissions can vary by Android version and app version. You can review or change app permissions in your device’s Android Settings. Revoking a permission may limit the related feature.

4. Location and world clocks

World clock times can be calculated using the time-zone information for the cities you select. If a version of EvoxaClock requests device location for a location-based feature, that information should be used only for the feature described in the permission prompt and its in-app explanation. This policy must be updated if location is collected, transmitted, or used in any additional way.

5. Sleep and bedtime features

Sleep or bedtime tools may use settings or information you provide to support the feature. Do not treat app-generated sleep estimates as medical advice or a clinical diagnosis. If a version connects to health platforms, sensors, or online services, the policy should clearly identify the information involved and how it is processed.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services only where those services are included in the installed version or where you explicitly choose to use an external Android feature. Review the privacy information for any such service. This page should be updated whenever a new third-party SDK or online feature is added.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored app settings and imported selections generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Android may retain system-managed alarm, notification, or permission records according to its own behavior. If online storage is added in a future version, this section must explain how to access and delete that data.

8. Children’s privacy

EvoxaClock is a general-purpose utility app and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been collected through an online feature, contact us so we can investigate.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the app’s design to protect information it handles. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keeping your device updated and protected helps safeguard locally stored information.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with a revised effective date.
